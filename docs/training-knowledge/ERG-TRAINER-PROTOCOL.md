# ERG Mode and Smart Trainer Protocol
## Zwift AI Coach

## What is ERG Mode
ERG mode = trainer controls resistance to maintain constant watts.
Rider controls cadence. Trainer adjusts resistance. Gear does NOT affect watts.

## Correct Setup Before Workout

### Gear Selection
- Middle of cassette: gear 4-6 out of 11
- Middle or large chainring
- NEVER change gear during ERG — causes spike and trainer confusion

### Target Cadence by Workout Type
- Zone 2: 85-95 RPM
- Sweet Spot: 88-95 RPM
- Threshold: 90-95 RPM
- VO2max: 95-105 RPM
- Sprint: 100-110 RPM
- Recovery: 80-90 RPM

## The Problem Described
"Heavy gear, crazy cadence, low watts" = gear too big.
Trainer reduced resistance to maintain watts → cadence spiked → spin out.

Solution: Always use middle gear. Cadence 88-95. Never change gear.

## What AI Coach Writes in Every Workout

Every workout description includes:
"ERG SETUP: Select gear 4-6 on cassette before starting.
Do not change gears during workout.
Target cadence: [X] RPM — trainer adjusts resistance automatically.
If cadence drops below 80 RPM, ease off and rebuild cadence before pushing watts."

Every interval block must include explicit cadenceTarget.

## FTP Must Match Between Zwift and AI
- Barak: ~215W (Coggan from 30 rides)
- To update: Zwift Settings → My Profile → FTP

## Diagnosis Protocol
1. Trainer recognized? → check pairing screen
2. ERG active? → ERG button highlighted
3. Cadence sensor working? → RPM displayed
4. FTP correct? → check Settings

## Iron Rule
Do not think about gears during ERG. Focus only on cadence.
The trainer does everything else.
